What is peer mental health support?

Peer support means students listening to students. It is not therapy and it does not replace professional care. It gives people a place to feel heard, trade coping ideas and find the next right step, including reaching out to a counsellor.

    Wellness resources

    These guides are general information. They do not replace professional care. If you are in crisis, contact a local emergency number or a qualified counsellor.

    Managing stress

    Try box breathing: in for 4, hold 4, out 4, hold 4. Repeat for two minutes before studying or an exam. Short walks also help.

    Balancing study

    Study in 45-minute blocks with 10-minute breaks. Plan the hardest subject for your best energy hours and protect sleep.

    Emotional wellbeing

    Name what you feel in one sentence, keep one regular routine, and stay in touch with someone you trust each day.

    Asking for help

    Reaching out is a strength. Start with a friend, mentor or campus counsellor, and say what you need: listening, advice or a plan.
